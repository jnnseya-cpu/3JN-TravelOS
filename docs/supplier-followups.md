# Supplier follow-ups — chase templates & realistic timelines

You've emailed the suppliers; no replies yet. That's normal for B2B travel
onboarding — it's slow, sales-gated, and usually needs a nudge. Use these.

## Realistic timelines (so you can plan, not just wait)
| Door | Typical time to live | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Travelpayouts** (benchmark) | **instant / self-serve** | Turn on **today** — token from travelpayouts.com → set `TRAVELPAYOUTS_TOKEN`. Not bookable, but it powers honest benchmarking + estimate calibration now. |
| **TBO Air** | ~1–3 weeks | Often *faster* than Travelfusion if you already have a TBO account — just ask them to enable the **Air (flights) API**. Good Gulf/long-haul. |
| **Travelfusion** | ~3–6 weeks | Sales-led + credit check + certification. The Ryanair/LCC unlock. |
| **Hotelbeds** | ~2–4 weeks | You're already keyed — this is *certification/contract*, not a new key (chase your account manager to enable live hotel availability). |
| **Duffel — better rates** | days | Ask your existing account manager (below) — sometimes a quick win. |

## 1) Chase — Travelfusion
> **Subject:** Following up — 3JN Travel OS Flight API application

> certification as soon as you can issue test credentials.
> Could you let me know the next step, any prefunding requirement, and a rough
> timeline? Happy to jump on a short call. Thank you — [name], 3JN Travel OS.

## 3) Chase — Hotelbeds (certification, not a key)
> **Subject:** Activate live hotel availability — 3JN Travel OS (keys already set)
>
> Hi [name/team],
> Our APItude keys authenticate (status OK) but live **hotel availability**
> returns 401 — which points to an entitlement/certification step rather than a
> credential issue. Could you confirm what's needed to enable live hotel content
> for our account (environment, source market, certification), and the timeline?
> Ready to complete certification now. Thanks — [name], 3JN Travel OS.

## 4) NEW — ask Duffel directly to lower your cost
This is the one you may not have tried, and it's the fastest lever on the exact
"Duffel too expensive" problem:
> **Subject:** Reducing our per-booking cost + access to lower fares — 3JN Travel OS
>
> Hi Duffel team,
> We're scaling on Duffel and want to improve price competitiveness on European
> short-haul. Two questions: (1) can our plan/per-order fee be reduced as volume
> grows, and (2) can we access **Duffel-negotiated / private fares** or add our
> own airline/consolidator deals into our Duffel account? Also keen on any
> lower-cost LCC content you can enable. Thanks — [name], 3JN Travel OS.
