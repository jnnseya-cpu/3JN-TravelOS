# 3JN Travel OS — Go-To-Market

**Fly home now. Pay monthly. Win the diaspora.**

Every giant OTA sells the same fare to everyone. 3JN sells **certainty** to the
one market they ignore: the African & Caribbean diaspora who travel home on a
fixed budget. Cheapest reliable package, price locked, spread over interest-free
instalments — plus an AI that tells you your visa odds before you book. This
document is the plan to get the first **100 paying customers in 90 days**, then
the flywheel that follows.

- **Launch market:** London → Lagos (UK Nigerian diaspora) — *locked, see §1*
- **Target:** 100 paid bookings in 90 days
- **Hook:** deposit + pay monthly, price locked

## 0. The wedge — don't boil the ocean

A new brand can't out-spend Skyscanner or Expedia on "cheap flights." It wins by
owning a community those giants treat as an afterthought — and being 10× better
for that one community before widening out.

| | |
|---|---|
| **The pain** | A £900 trip home, and no £900 spare. Fares spike exactly when everyone needs to fly (December, weddings, funerals). Cash-flow, not desire, is the blocker. |
| **The unlock** | Pay monthly, price locked. Small deposit today, the rest spread interest-free, the fare frozen from day one. The AI even buys and **holds** the ticket the moment payments cover it. Nobody selling to this market does this. |
| **The moat** | Trust + visa certainty. VisaOS tells you your approval odds before you spend a penny and issues real cancellable reservations for the application — something price comparison can't copy. |

## 1. Launch city — LOCKED: London → Lagos

We launch in **one** market and dominate it before widening. That market is the
**UK Nigerian diaspora, London-first, flagship route London → Lagos (LOS)**.

### Why Lagos first

- **Largest addressable community.** The UK's Nigerian diaspora is the biggest
  West-African group, heavily concentrated in London (Peckham, Woolwich, Barking
  & Dagenham, Thamesmead, Croydon) plus strong pockets in Manchester &
  Birmingham for phase two.
- **Highest-urgency, highest-fare travel.** "Detty December", weddings, funerals
  and owambe drive non-optional trips at £600–£1,200+ — exactly where
  pay-monthly + price-lock is most valuable and most differentiated.
- **The product already points here.** `/flights/london-to-lagos` and
  `/destinations/lagos` are live, indexable pages — the SEO net is already cast.
- **Dense, reachable community channels.** Nigerian WhatsApp/Facebook groups,
  churches (RCCG, Winners), town/alumni unions, and creators are numerous, active
  and trust-driven — perfect for the founder-led first 100.

### Launch-city focus (first 90 days)

- **Primary route:** London (LHR/LGW) → Lagos (LOS). Secondary: London → Abuja (ABV).
- **Where the customers are:** South-East London (Peckham/Woolwich), Barking &
  Dagenham, Croydon — plus online Nigerian-diaspora groups nationwide.
- **On-the-ground nodes:** African grocers & money-transfer shops on Rye Lane,
  churches, Nigerian restaurants/lounges, alumni & town-union meetups.

### Expansion sequence (only after Lagos is humming)

1. **London → Accra (ACC)** — Ghanaian diaspora, same playbook, adjacent community.
2. **London → Kingston (KIN)** — Caribbean diaspora, big-occasion + VFR.
3. **London → Kinshasa (FIH)** — under-served, low competition, high loyalty.

> Swappable: if you have a stronger personal network in the Ghanaian or Caribbean
> community, lead with Accra or Kingston instead — the plan and budget are
> identical, only the route pages and community channels change.

## 2. Customer segments — four buyers, one feeling: "I can finally afford to go"

Rank by urgency and ticket size. Chase the top two first — they convert fastest
and refer hardest. Ignore the anti-personas until the engine hums.

| Segment | Why they buy | Economics |
|---|---|---|
| **Homecoming traveller (VFR)** — *primary* | Visits family 1–2×/yr. Pay-monthly is the whole reason they say yes. December is their Black Friday. | £600–£1,200 · price-driven |
| **Big-occasion flyer** — *primary* | Weddings, funerals, owambe, graduations. Non-negotiable, often a group, often last-minute. | Group of 3–8 · high AOV |
| **Visa-anxious traveller** — *secondary* | Students, family reunification, first-timers who fear rejection. VisaOS is a free reason to arrive; the trip is the upsell. | Visa-led · sticky |

**Don't chase yet:** generic OTA price-shoppers, luxury, and corporate. Expensive
to win, disloyal, and they dilute the brand while it's still being built. *Focus
is the strategy.*

## 3. The first 100 customers — do things that don't scale, on purpose

The first 100 come from people who already trust *you*, not from an algorithm.
Founder-led, high-touch, unglamorous — and the highest-ROI work you'll ever do.
Ranked by speed-to-first-sale:

1. **Your network + Nigerian WhatsApp/Facebook groups** — post in the 5–8 groups
   you're genuinely part of, with the route link that fits (`/flights/london-to-lagos`).
   *1 day → 10–25 warm leads → 5–12 booked.*
2. **The concierge close (white-glove, manual)** — DM/call 50 people planning a
   trip home; don't send a link, **book it for them** over WhatsApp (quote →
   deposit → done), fulfilled via Curated Deals. This is where trust is minted.
   *Ongoing → 50 warm → 15–25 paid.*
3. **Community gatekeepers & micro-influencers** — pastors, association chairs,
   event MCs, UK-Naija creators (5k–50k). One trusted post out-converts £500 of
   cold ads. *1–2 wks → 3–5 partners → 20+ sales.*
4. **Remittance adjacency** — people sending money home *are* your buyers.
   Partner with a money-transfer shop for a referral cut. *2 wks → recurring trickle.*
5. **Physical presence** — African grocers, salons, barbershops, churches. Flyer
   + QR to `/destinations/lagos`. *Slow burn + brand lift.*
6. **Events & the December wave** — Afrobeats nights, diaspora festivals,
   end-of-year events. "Book December, pay monthly" table/promo. *Seasonal spikes, big AOV.*

**The funnel that carries them:** no-signup AI search → email captured → welcome
+ pay-monthly offer → concierge close on WhatsApp → **ask for the referral the
day they book.** Every happy traveller is your next three customers.

## 4. Suppliers & how to source them

The platform is fail-closed: each supplier is a credential-gated "door" that
lights up when its keys are set. Sell through what's wired, earn while you
integrate the rest, and never front cash you don't have.

| Door | Supplier | Role | Status | How to source it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flights | **Duffel** | Live fares + ticketing + Stays | key-ready | Self-serve signup → test token now → KYC for live token. Integrated. |
| Flights | TBO / Kiwi (Tequila) | Consolidator + LCC breadth | outreach | Apply for partner API; TBO enablement email drafted. Ops-desk ticketing to start. |
| Hotels | **Hotelbeds (APItude)** | Bedbank — core supply | key-ready | Apply, sign, sandbox → production. Prepaid avoids bonding early. |
| Hotels | Amadeus / WebBeds | Fallback + breadth | roadmap | Amadeus self-service keys; WebBeds partner application. Add on volume. |
| Payments | **Stripe** | Card checkout + instalments | key-ready | Live keys + webhook. Foundation for deposit + pay-monthly. |
| eSIM | Airalo / eSIMAccess | Land-with-data attach | key-ready | Partner signup; hourly catalogue sync built. High margin. |
| Extras | Viator · Mozio · CarTrawler | Activities + transfers | key-ready | Affiliate/partner signups. Pure-margin add-ons. |
| Insurance | XCover / Battleface | Trip protection attach | roadmap | Partner API + authorisation flag. Attach at checkout. |
| **Protection** | **TTA → ATOL** | Financial protection (legal) | **apply now** | The gate for flight packages. TTA trust-account membership first (fast); ATOL after. |

**Sell on day one — no full stack needed:** publish **Curated Deals** (real
packages at a real all-in price, fulfilled manually through your agent network,
payable exactly like a live fare) and earn **affiliate** revenue (TravelPayouts,
Viator) while APIs are still in sandbox. Every concierge sale runs through this —
revenue from week one.

**The one thing you must not skip — financial protection.** Selling
flight-inclusive packages to UK travellers **legally requires** protection and
it's your #1 trust signal. **Apply to a TTA membership now** (trust-account
model, faster than ATOL); pursue ATOL Small Business in parallel. Until certified,
sell what you can today (hotels, visa reservations, eSIM, curated deals). The
protection badge is wired site-wide and stays hidden until it's real.

**Sourcing playbook for any new supplier:** apply for sandbox → build against it →
request production + credit terms → negotiate net rates/commission → sign. *Start
prepaid* to avoid bonding, then earn credit terms once volume is proven.

### 7.2 — Unit economics (worked model)

*Assumptions — adjust to your real mix:*

| Metric | Value | Basis |
|---|---:|---|
| Average order value (AOV) | **£850** | Flight ~£620 + hotel 3–4 nts ~£180 + eSIM/extras ~£50 |
| Blended net take rate | **~10%** | Flight commission 2–4% + hotel bedbank margin 10–15% + savings-share + attach |
| **Contribution per booking** | **~£85** | AOV × take rate, net of Stripe fees (~1.5%+20p UK / ~2.5%+20p intl) |
| Trips per customer (lifetime) | **2.5** | Diaspora repeat + referral behaviour |
| **Base LTV (contribution)** | **~£210** | £85 × 2.5, before referral uplift |
| **Target CAC ceiling** | **≤ £70** | For LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1 |
| **Blended CAC target** | **£30–£50** | Organic + referral pull the average down |
| **CAC payback** | **< 60 days** | First trip's contribution covers acquisition |

### 7.3 — Does 100 bookings pay for itself?

- 100 bookings × ~£85 contribution ≈ **£8,500 gross contribution** in 90 days.
- Against ~£10k–£11k lean cash spend → **roughly cash-neutral in the first 90
  days**, *and* you've built the compounding assets (SEO surface, email list,
  referral loop, reviews) that make month 4+ profitable.
- **Honest framing:** the first 90 days are **invest-to-build**, not profit.
  Success = 100 paid, proven unit economics on ≥1 channel, and a growing list —
  not a Q1 profit line.
- **Sensitivity:** raise AOV (more hotel nights, insurance/eSIM attach) or lift
  the referral coefficient and this flips clearly profitable; let paid CAC run
  above £85 and it doesn't. Kill any channel above contribution margin; scale only
  what clears payback.
